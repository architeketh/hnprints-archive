One-button form filler

Fills in the seller "add product" form for any hnPrints piece. It never clicks Save for you.

Step 1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome or Edge.

Step 2. Drag this button up to the bookmarks bar and let go:

hnPrints Fill

If dragging doesn't work, use the copy option below.

How to use it

  1. Sign in to the seller site and open the Add product page: info-748.sp-seller.webkul.com
  2. Click the hnPrints Fill bookmark. A small panel appears in the top-right corner.
  3. Type part of a piece's name, or its number. Press the down arrow to move through matches.
  4. Press Enter. The form fills in: title, description, price, quantity, tags, your collections, USPS shipping, and the images. The panel shows a ✓ or ✗ for each item.
  5. Look the form over, then click the site's own Save button.
  6. Back in the panel, click Mark as added so you can see which pieces you've finished. Then type the next name.
If dragging doesn't work

Click Copy, then add a new bookmark by hand: right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it hnPrints Fill, and paste this into the URL box.

If something doesn't fill

Open the panel's Settings and click Save form structure file. Send me the file it downloads. It lists the names of the form's boxes only, nothing you typed, and I'll adjust the filler to match.
